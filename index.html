Road Safety Awareness & Traffic Management

Road Safety Awareness

🪖 Helmet Safety

Always wear an ISI-certified helmet while riding.

🚗 Seat Belt Safety

Seat belts significantly reduce injuries during accidents.

🚦 Traffic Signals

Follow Red, Yellow and Green traffic signal indications.

🚸 Zebra Crossing

Give priority to pedestrians at zebra crossings.

Vehicle Documents

📄 Driving License

Mandatory document to operate a motor vehicle.

📘 RC Book

Vehicle registration certificate issued by RTO.

🛡 Insurance

Third-party vehicle insurance is compulsory.

🌱 PUC Certificate

Pollution Under Control certificate is mandatory.

Traffic Rules

🚦 Signal Rules

Red = Stop | Yellow = Wait | Green = Go

📵 Mobile Usage

Do not use mobile phones while driving.

🚗 Speed Limits

Follow speed limits as prescribed.

🍺 No Drunk Driving

Never drive under the influence of alcohol.
