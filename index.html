Road Safety Awareness

Wear Helmet

Always wear ISI-certified helmets while riding two-wheelers.

Use Seat Belt

Seat belts reduce fatal injuries significantly during collisions.

Follow Signals

Obey traffic lights and lane discipline.

Important Traffic Rules

Speed Limits

Follow prescribed speed limits for highways and city roads.

No Mobile Usage

Using mobile phones while driving is prohibited.

Drink & Drive

Driving under alcohol influence is a punishable offence.

Lane Discipline

Stay in designated lanes and use indicators properly.
